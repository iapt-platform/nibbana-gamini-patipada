## 身随观(kāyānupassanā) 安那般那节(ānāpānapabba) 巴利圣典

1. Kathañca bhikkhave bhikkhu kāye kāyānupassī viharati.

Idha bhikkhave bhikkhu araññagato vā rukkhamūlagato vā suññāgāragato vā nisīdati pallaṅkaṃ ābhujitvā ujuṃ kāyaṃ paṇidhāya parimukhaṃ satiṃ upaṭṭhapetvā. 
So satova assasati, satova passasati.

2. Dīghaṃ vā assasanto “dīghaṃ assasāmī”ti pajānāti.<br>Dīghaṃ vā passasanto “dīghaṃ passasāmī”ti pajānāti.<br><br>Rassaṃ vā assasanto “rassaṃ assasāmī”ti pajānāti.<br>Rassaṃ vā passasanto “rassaṃ passasāmī”ti pajānāti.<br><br>“Sabbakāyapaṭisaṃvedī assasissāmī”ti sikkhati.<br>“Sabbakāyapaṭisaṃvedī passasissāmī”ti sikkhati.<br><br>“Passambhayaṃ kāyasaṅkhāraṃ assasissāmī”ti sikkhati.<br>“Passambhayaṃ kāyasaṅkhāraṃ passasissāmī”ti sikkhati.

3. Seyyathāpi bhikkhave dakkho bhamakāro vā bhamakārantevāsī vā dīghaṃ vā añchanto “dīghaṃ añchāmī”ti pajānāti, rassaṃ vā añchanto “rassaṃ añchāmī”ti pajānāti. 
Evameva kho bhikkhave bhikkhu dīghaṃ vā assasanto “dīghaṃ assasāmī”ti pajānāti. 
Dīghaṃ vā passasanto “dīghaṃ passasāmī”ti pajānāti. 
Rassaṃ vā assasanto “rassaṃ assasāmī”ti pajānāti. 
Rassaṃ vā passasanto “rassaṃ passasāmī”ti pajānāti. 
“Sabbakāya-paṭisaṃvedī assasissāmī”ti sikkhati, “sabbakāyapaṭisaṃvedī passasissāmī”ti sikkhati. 
“Passambhayaṃ kāyasaṅkhāraṃ assasissāmī”ti sikkhati, “passambhayaṃ kāyasaṅkhāraṃ passasissāmī”ti sikkhati.

4. Iti ajjhattaṃ vā kāye kāyānupassī viharati, bahiddhā vā kāye kāyānupassī viharati, ajjhattabahiddhā vā kāye kāyānupassī viharati.

5. Samudayadhammānupassī vā kāyasmiṃ viharati, vayadhammānupassī vā kāyasmiṃ viharati, samudaya-vayadhammānupassī vā kāyasmiṃ viharati.

6. “Atthi kāyo”ti vā panassa sati paccupaṭṭhitā hoti yāvadeva ñāṇamattāya paṭissatimattāya.
Anissito ca viharati, na ca kiñci loke upādiyati. 
Evampi kho bhikkhave bhikkhu kāye kāyānupassī viharati. (Ma.1.70-71.)
