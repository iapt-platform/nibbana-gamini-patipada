## 依烦恼轮(kilesavaṭṭa)、业轮(kammavaṭṭa)、异熟轮(vipākavaṭṭa)之力的收摄方式

Aparo ----

- 1. “Purimakammabhavasmiṃ moho avijjā, āyūhanā saṅkhārā, nikanti taṇhā, upagamanaṃ upādānaṃ, cetanā bhavoti ime pañca dhammā purimakammabhavasmiṃ idha paṭisandhiyā paccayā.
- 2. Idha paṭisandhi viññāṇaṃ, okkanti nāmarūpaṃ, pasādo āyatanaṃ, phuṭṭho phasso, vedayitaṃ vedanāti ime pañca dhammā idhūpapattibhavasmiṃ purekatassa kammassa paccayā.
- 3. Idha paripakkattā āyatanānaṃ moho avijjā, āyūhanā saṅkhārā, nikanti taṇhā, upagamanaṃ upādānaṃ, cetanā bhavoti ime pañca dhammā idha kammabhavasmiṃ āyatiṃ paṭisandhiyā paccayā”ti evaṃ **kammavaṭṭavipākavaṭṭavasena** nāmarūpassa paccayapariggahaṃ karoti. (Visuddhi. 2. 235.) (Paṭisaṃ. 50.)

Kammasahāyattā kilesavaṭṭaṃ kammavaṭṭe pakkhipitvā vuttaṃ “**kammavaṭṭavipākavaṭṭavasenā**”ti. (Mahāṭī. 2. 376.)

另一位瑜伽行(yogāvacara)者则以**业轮、异熟轮**之力，另一种方式即以**烦恼轮、业轮**、异熟轮之力，来收摄名色(nāmarūpa)的因法。 
（应记住：由于烦恼轮是业(kamma)的伴侣，义注(aṭṭhakathā)注释师便把烦恼轮纳入业轮之中，从而解说为「以业轮、异熟轮之力」。）
